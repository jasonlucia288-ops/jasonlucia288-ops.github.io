# ClipBot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

ClipBot is a personal, single-user tool run by the creator behind the "Habibi" (@american_habibi) gaming channels. It edits the creator's own gameplay recordings into short videos and publishes them to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts through those platforms' official APIs. ClipBot is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## What data ClipBot accesses

When the account owner signs in and grants permission, ClipBot accesses only what it needs to post and measure the owner's own videos:

* **YouTube (Google):** permission to upload videos to the owner's channel, read the owner's channel and video details, read live chat of the owner's own live streams, and read YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's videos.
* **TikTok:** the owner's basic profile info, and permission to upload videos as drafts or posts to the owner's account and read the owner's video statistics.
* **Instagram / Facebook (Meta):** the owner's Instagram professional account and linked Facebook Page, permission to publish videos to them and read their insights.

ClipBot does not access data about any other person, does not read private messages, and does not collect data from viewers.

## How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to show the owner how they perform, so ClipBot can pick better clips. Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own Windows PC using Windows' built-in credential protection. Video statistics are stored in a local database on that PC. Nothing is sent to any server other than the official API of the platform involved. ClipBot uses only local AI models; no data is sent to third-party AI services.

## Sharing

ClipBot does not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. There are no advertising or analytics trackers.

## Google API Services

ClipBot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. ClipBot uses YouTube API Services; see the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data retention and deletion {#data-deletion}

Raw recordings and working files are deleted automatically after 7 days. To delete everything ClipBot holds, the owner deletes the local ClipBot folder and its saved credentials. To revoke access at any time:

* Google / YouTube: [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)
* TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services
* Meta: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites, or Instagram Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites

After access is revoked, ClipBot deletes the stored tokens and statistics for that platform the next time it runs. Anyone who believes ClipBot holds data about them can ask for deletion through the contact link below, and it will be removed within 30 days.
